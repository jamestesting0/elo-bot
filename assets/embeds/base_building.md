The Elo system is not only made for attackers but also for the **base builders!** You may submit your bases as a "helper's base", to have the Elo bot to track their performance in Elo matches. Then you can check your bases' ranking and statistics with the following commands:
- `/base info`
- `/base leaderboard`
- `/season base leaderboard`

**How to participate?**
1. Submit your base through `/base submit` command.
2. The Elo managers will review your base, and you will be notified once it is accepted or rejected.

**What are the criteria for the bases?**
1. Any quality of bases works!
2. You may submit your own modded version of famous.
   -  However, please do not submit bases that are 90+% identical.
