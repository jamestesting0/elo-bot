**Season Cycle**
Each season lasts two months. The next season begins on the first Monday of the following month, and matches not completed by then are deleted.
*`/match queue` closes at the start of the season's final Raid Weekend (Friday).*

**Elo Final**
The 8 players with the highest Elo are selected on the Friday of the season's second-to-last week and seeded by Elo.
• **Quarterfinals:** about 4 days
• **Semifinals:** about 3 more days
• **Final:** before the next season begins

These durations are approximate and may change.
