**Starting Your First Match**
1. Click on the **"Participate"** button.
2. Select your preferred districts once an opponent is found.
3. Once in a match, a 1v1 helper will guide you. 

**FAQ**
"Are the matches time-consuming?"
> No, we feature "war-style" matches, where players attack independently. Meaning you can do your attacks anytime and do not have to wait for your opponent's attack!

"What are some useful commands?"
> 1. `/match queue` to find a match. 
> 2. `/challenge send` to challenge someone to a match.
