**Districts**
Every match has one district from each group:
1. Barbarian Camp, Skeleton Park, or Wizard Valley (if both players are under 1200 Elo)
2. Balloon Lagoon, Builder's Workshop, Golem Quarry
3. Dragon Cliffs, Goblin Mines, or Capital Peak (if both players enable it with `/player capital-peak`)

**Submitting Your Bases**
You can use your own bases when both players have 1100+ Elo.
- `/match base submit`: submit a base link and screenshot
- `/match base view`: view submitted bases
- `/match base remove` or `/match base remove-all`: remove submissions
- `/preset base add`: automatically submit a base for that district in future matches
- `/preset base view` or `/preset base remove`: manage presets

*Submit within 12 hours of match creation. Any district without a submission uses a helper base.*

**Helper Bases**
The bot picks community bases based on Elo:
- **Average:** under 1000 Elo
- **Good:** 1000-1099 Elo
- **Great:** 1100-1199 Elo
- **Excellent:** 1200+ Elo

*To contribute bases, use **Base Building** on the Elo information post.*
