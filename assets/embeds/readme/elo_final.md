Follow the Elo Final in <#1392523333043617992>.

**Selection**
- Top 8 by Elo get a DM invite that expires after 24 hours.
- Seeds 1–3 pick quarterfinal opponents from seeds 5–8 in order. Seed 4 gets the remaining opponent.
- Allow about 4 days for quarterfinals and 3 for semifinals. The final is before next season.

**Match Thread**
- Submit links with `/match base submit` and review them with `/match base view`.
- Scout by district with `/match base scout` from 30 minutes before your start.
- Play 7 districts. Staff arrange a district replay for ties.
- Managers use `/final match result` in the thread and `/final match forfeit` to advance the forfeiting player’s opponent.

**Predictions**
- Pick the bracket with **Predict** or `/final predict` before predictions close.
- After closing, `/final predictions view` ranks players by correct picks. Each correct pick pays 400 <:token:1555865080711876660> at the end.

**Tokens**
- Registered players get 5,000 <:token:1555865080711876660> once. Check `/tokens balance` or `/tokens history`.
- Use `/match stake` before either player starts. Match the offer or go `all-in`. If your all-in is below half the offer, both stakes drop to your amount. The winner of a played match takes the pot.

**Betting**
- Use **Place / Edit Bet** when the round opens. Edit or cancel until the match starts or staff close betting. You can’t bet on your own match.
- Winners split the pool in proportion to their bets. Everyone gets a refund if the match is refunded or nobody backs the winner.
- A corrected result can create debt if you spent the payout. Debt blocks bet increases and switches.

**Useful Commands**
- `/final bracket view`: current bracket
- `/final profile`: a player's Final results and betting record
- `/final matches`: Final results
