**Selection**
The top 8 players receive DM invitations on the selection date. Invitations expire after 24 hours. Players are seeded by Elo.
- **Quarterfinals:** Seed 1 picks an opponent from seeds 5 to 8, then seeds 2 and 3 choose from those left. Seed 4 faces the remaining player. About 4 days.
- **Semifinals:** about 3 more days
- **Final:** before the next season begins

*Final matches use 7 districts. If the total score is tied, staff arrange a district replay.*

**Scouting**
From 30 minutes before your scheduled start, use `/match base scout` in the match thread. Choose a district to see your opponent's scout image.

**Match Thread**
Use `/match base submit` to submit a base link and `/match base view` to review your bases. Managers can submit or correct scores with `/final match result` in the thread.

Managers can use `/final match forfeit` to select the forfeiting player, enter a reason and confirm that their opponent advances.

**Predictions**
Use **Predict** on the pinned bracket post or `/final predict` to pick winners for the whole bracket. Submit and edit your picks until predictions close, before play begins. Once predictions close, `/final predictions view` shows everyone's picks, sorted by correct picks. A score like `5/7` shows five correct picks across seven scored matches. Each correct pick pays 400 <:token:1555865080711876660> when the Final ends.

**Tokens**
Registered players get 5,000 <:token:1555865080711876660> once. Use `/tokens balance` to check your tokens and `/tokens history` for wallet activity.

Final opponents can use `/match stake` before either player starts. Enter the same amount to match an offer, or use `all-in` to use your full available balance. If your all-in covers at least half of a larger offer, both amounts stand. Below half, both stakes lock at your amount. The winner takes both locked stakes after a played result.

**Betting**
Betting posts appear when a round starts. Use **Place / Edit Bet** to back a player.
- Winning bets split the whole pool by stake. If nobody backed the winner or the match is refunded, every stake is returned.
- Change, switch or cancel your bet until betting closes. It closes when the match starts, or earlier if staff close it.
- Players cannot bet on their own match.
- If a result correction reverses a payout you already spent, the difference becomes debt. You cannot increase or switch a bet while in debt.

**Useful Commands**
- `/final bracket view`: current bracket
- `/final profile`: a player's Final results and betting record
- `/final matches`: Final results
