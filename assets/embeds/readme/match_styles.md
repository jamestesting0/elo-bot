**How a Match Works**
Both players attack the same 3 districts, with 2 attacks per district. Total stars decide the winner, then total destruction. Equal scores are a draw.

**War Style (Default)**
Players attack separately, and your opponent's score stays hidden until you finish.
- Ping the 1v1 helpers in your match thread when ready, then stay around for 20 minutes. To schedule ahead, post a Discord timestamp.
- Friendly challenges expire after 30 minutes, so start every district within that time.
- Finish your attacks within 48 hours of your opponent attacking.

**Live Style**
Players take turns in the same clan, with spectators allowed. Agree on a time in your match thread for a helper to host.
- Your registered account must be TH5 or below.
- Do not take more than 3 minutes between your attacks.
- During the raid weekend, live matches need both players' bases stored in the same clan before it starts.

*You can also agree to attack simultaneously. Both players attack at the same time, without the time limit between attacks.*

**Challenges**
Use `/challenge send` to choose your opponent.
- You can send up to 2 challenges per week.
- Both players need an unused normal ranked match from the past 7 days. The challenge uses one from each player.
- You cannot challenge the same player twice in a row.
- Wins give 0 to +35 Elo; losses cost 0 to 30 Elo.

Use `/challenge disable` or `/challenge enable` for all incoming challenges, and `/challenge block` or `/challenge unblock` for one player.

**Raid Weekend**
Ask helpers to store your bases before the raid weekend if you want to play during it. Raid weekend time does not count towards the time limits in Rules & Penalties.
