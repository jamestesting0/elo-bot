**Minor Penalty (-50%)**
1. Disconnecting or leaving an attack after deploying 20- housing spaces of troops.
2. \*Taking 3+ minutes between attacks in a live match.
3. \*Not submitting bases within 12 hours of match creation, if you intended to submit and your opponent waited for them.

**Major Penalty (-1 Star 50%)**
1. Using more than 3 miners in an attack.
2. Disconnecting or leaving an attack after deploying 20+ housing spaces of troops, if you choose to redo it.
3. Causing unnecessary drama.

**Invalid Score (0 Stars 0%)**
1. Not finishing a district within 30 minutes in a war-style match.
2. \*Disappearing during a live match without a valid reason.
3. Severely disrupting the match, such as flooding chat to hide replays.

**Autowin (+5/-35 Elo)**
1. \*Arriving 20+ minutes late to a live match.
2. Not attacking within 48 hours of your opponent's attacks. You get 12 more hours if you said in the match thread that you would attack soon.

**Match Cancelled**
1. Both players fail to attack within 48 hours.

**Disconnections & Redos**
1. An attack does not count if nothing was deployed, or if your troops and spells dealt no damage, took no damage and crossed no potential traps. Ask the helper to verify a redo.
2. For an early disconnect on your first attack (under 20 housing spaces and at most 1 spell), ask the helper to resend the challenge. Repeat the same plan; changing it may earn a minor penalty.
3. If your spells expire after a disconnect between attacks, replace them in the same spots and tell the helper.
4. If you deployed a larger part of your army, the attack counts as it is.
5. If maintenance or another game interruption prevents your second attack, you can redo it. Your opponent may keep the base or choose a different helper base.

*Raid weekend time does not count. Your opponent decides whether to apply penalties marked with "\*".*
