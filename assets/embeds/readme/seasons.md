**Season Cycle**
- Seasons last about two months.
- `/match queue` closes when the season's last raid weekend begins.
- The season ends the following Monday, when the raid weekend finishes. Unfinished matches are deleted then.

*If the Elo Final has not started 3 days after selection, the season extends to the next season end. All dates move with it.*

**Season Reset**
At reset, you keep your floor plus 60% of the Elo above it, capped at 1400 Elo. Your floor is:
- **1250:** Elo above 1300
- **1150:** Highest Elo of 1200+
- **1050:** Highest Elo above 1150
- **950:** everyone else
